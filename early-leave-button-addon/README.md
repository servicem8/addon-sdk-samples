# Early Leave Button Addon

A fun ServiceM8 addon that lets team members try their luck at leaving early on Friday afternoons. The earlier in the day you try, the lower your chances of success!

## Features

- Interactive button with a 5-second countdown
- Time-based probability calculation
- Responsive and modern UI design
- Available as a company-wide action in ServiceM8

## Installation

1. Install the addon through the ServiceM8 Addon Store
2. Access the Early Leave Button from the company actions menu
3. Try your luck!

## Usage

1. Open the Early Leave Button on Friday afternoon
2. Click the "Press Your Luck!" button
3. Wait for the 5-second countdown
4. See if you're lucky enough to leave early!

Note: The probability of success increases as the day progresses.
